The recommendation

Make admin.kaspasejo.com the control room, not another database.

Keep the ownership line simple: the vault remembers, the CRM runs work, app databases remain authoritative for their own customers, and Kai is the operator across them.

Phase 2.5: put Kai inside the admin after the CRM core works, before replacing Attio. The panel can start read-only, then gain reviewed actions.

What lands under one login

CRM: Today, People, Pipeline, Work and email context.

Apps: users, plans, promo codes, broadcasts, subscriptions and audit.

Kai: chat, evidence, proposed actions and approval.

One sign-in

No per-seat CRM

Attio can become archive or be cancelled.

Email inside admin

Context in Admin. Reading and sending stay in Gmail.

The CRM explains the relationship around an email. Gmail remains the inbox and composer.

BUILD IN ADMIN

• Read-only thread context on each person • Sender, recipients, subject, attachments and canonical Gmail link • Status, next step and next-action date inferred from activity • Kai proposes a draft with a copy-to-clipboard button • Every inferred field shows its source and confidence

DO NOT REBUILD

• No second inbox, folders or unread state • No competing Gmail composer • No hidden send button inside the CRM • No copied attachment store without a clear need • No claim of automation before Gmail activity is observable

THE HONEST ACCESS LINE

Auto-updates and reply detection need one of two inputs: the Gmail companion can observe the open Gmail tab, or a proper Gmail connection can supply reliable mailbox events. Without either, Admin cannot know a send or reply happened. This must be visible in settings and onboarding, not buried as a technical dependency.

Sources: Sam voice note, 11 Sep 2026; Attio email/calendar sync: attio.com/help/reference/email-calendar/email-and-calendar-syncingADMIN.KASPASEJO.COM · 12
Activity automation

A conversation should move the record forward.

Use evidence first, then update only what the email supports.

01 · OBSERVE

Send, reply or inbound email arrives through the companion or Gmail connection.

02 · INTERPRET

Match the person and brand. Extract intent, promised action, owner and date.

03 · PROPOSE

Show the status change, next step and due date with the exact email as evidence.

04 · APPLY

Low-risk fields can update automatically. Ambiguous or high-impact changes enter review.

EXAMPLE: OUTBOUND

“Can you send the demo metrics by Friday?” Status → Waiting on contact. Next step → Review demo metrics. Due → Friday.

EXAMPLE: INBOUND

“I’ll get those over next Tuesday.” Status → Promised. Next step → Await metrics. Due → Next Tuesday.

WHEN IT IS UNCLEAR

“Let’s catch up soon.” No invented date. Keep the current status and add a review card to Today.

Design rule: an automation must retain the source event, prior value, new value and rollback path.ADMIN.KASPASEJO.COM · 13
Sequences

Two modes. One sequence model. No hidden requirement.

MODE A · GMAIL CONNECTED

Day 1 sends through the connected Gmail account. Day 7 sends only if no reply is detected. Pauses on reply, bounce, unsubscribe or manual stop. Every send is visible in the contact timeline and audit log.

MODE B · NO GMAIL CONNECTION

Day 1: Kai prepares a draft and puts it in Today. Sam copies it into Gmail. Day 7: Kai prepares the follow-up and reminder. Reply suppression works only if the companion has observed the thread; otherwise Sam confirms “replied” or “send next step”.

Stop conditions

Reply · bounce · unsubscribe · deal closed · manual pause

Guardrails

Per-brand sending identity · working hours · daily cap · audit

First template

Day 1 outreach · Day 7 follow-up · no branching in v1

What changes in Kai

Kai is the assistant inside Admin.

Product name: Admin. Assistant name: Kai. The distinction is now explicit.

READ-ONLY FIRST

Ask about people, pipelines, promises, emails and app state. Every answer links back to its evidence.

DRAFT, THEN COPY

Kai writes the email in Sam’s voice. “Copy draft” puts the exact text on the clipboard; Sam pastes and sends in Gmail.

BULK ACTIONS

Segment, tag, assign, set next steps, prepare sequence enrolments and export. Show the proposed set before applying.

REVIEWED ACTIONS LATER

Once the read model is trusted, Kai can execute approved CRM changes. External communication remains separately controlled.

ATTIO MIGRATION CHANGE

There is no Attio history worth importing. Remove Attio from the migration path. Admin supersedes the future need for it; the source systems are the Master CRM sheet, Gmail/companion activity, meetings and the app databases.
