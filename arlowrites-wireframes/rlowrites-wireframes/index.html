Review principles

  • Show channel and publish time before approval.
  • Explain why the draft sounds like the founder.
  • Separate “change this draft” from “change my voice”.
  • Never publish from a casual swipe without a clear undo state.
  • Keep one draft, one decision and one visible next action.

System notes

Design and build direction

The visual system should feel like quiet expert help: warm, precise and ready before the founder arrives.

Visual language

Ink #20231DCanvas #F7F7F2Signal #E8F25AInfo #DBE8F4

Typography

Use a modern grotesk with clear weights. Large headings stay on two intentional lines. Body measure: 45-70 characters. No orphan words, manual line breaks or narrow copy columns inside wide frames.

Motion

Light spring transitions, queue cards that settle rather than fly, and a visible confirmation trail. Respect reduced-motion settings.

Accessibility

44px minimum touch targets, status conveyed by label and colour, clear focus states, AA contrast and readable previews at 100% zoom.

Product rules

Concierge before configuration.
Every screen leads with what Arlo prepared and the one decision the founder needs to make.
Voice learning is explicit.
Show what Arlo inferred, let the founder correct it and explain when an edit changes future writing.
Approval has a boundary.
Always show channel, publish time and scope. Keep undo and pause within reach.
Performance ends in a recommendation.
Translate channel numbers into one evidence-backed change for the next plan.
Naming decision.
Arlowrites is the working brand. Domain availability must be checked again before purchase.
