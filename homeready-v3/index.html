The official checklist

What the Government says every home should have

  • Battery or wind-up torch (safer than candles)
  • Battery or wind-up radio, for updates in a power cut
  • Portable power bank for your phone + spare batteries
  • First aid kit, hand sanitiser and wet wipes
  • Bottled water - 2.5-3 litres per person per day (WHO guidance)
  • Non-perishable food that doesn't need cooking, plus a tin opener

In a power cut, dial 105 to report it (England, Scotland and Wales). Write important phone numbers down on paper - your mobile may be dead.
