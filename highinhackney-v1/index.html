Straight answers

Is this legal?

Yes. This is a mailing list, nothing more. Cannabis is a Class B drug and psilocybin is a Class A drug in the UK. Selling or possessing either is illegal today, and we do neither.

What exactly am I joining?

A queue. If and when the law changes, members find out first what High in Hackney becomes, and get first access to it.

Are you selling weed?

No. There is no product, no shop and no preorder. Nothing changes hands here until the law says it can.

Why do you want my number?

The day the law changes will move fast. A text reaches you when an email doesn't.

So when will it be legal?

Nobody honest can give you a date. Medical cannabis has been legal in the UK since 2018, Canada and Germany have gone further, and the debate in Parliament keeps moving. We watch it full time.

What happens to my details?

They're used for this waitlist and nothing else. No ads, no resale, no third parties. Ask to be removed and you're gone. The short version is on the privacy page.
